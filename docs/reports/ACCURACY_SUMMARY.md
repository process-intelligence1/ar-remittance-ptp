## Confidence formula
Invoice match 35 %, amount 30 %, sender-to-company code 10 %, name 10 %, date 5 % (raw ÷ 0.90 = formula confidence); a fast path (exact invoice number, open item, same currency, amount within 2 %) gives at least 92 %. The confidence is the higher of the two, and 80 % or more with a BSID match is auto-processed. The Knowledge Base worked example is reproduced exactly (no match 56.4 %, OCR variant 83.7 %, exact number 95.3 %).

## Known weak spots
- Short payments (R03, R07, R27, P08 instalments) and the five exception scenarios keep the strict line accuracy below target.
- Some tilted scans are not straightened (OpenCV angle handling); the affected numbers go to review.
- Not covered by any sample, tested by unit tests only: exchange rates in remittances, REM- references, relative promise dates, credit-note formats, IBAN payers, matching by name.
